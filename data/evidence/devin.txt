How quotas work
Pro and Max include a usage allowance that refreshes automatically on a calendar basis:
Pro has a daily and weekly allowance. The daily allowance is more than 1/7 of the weekly, so you can keep working through weekends without giving up overall capacity for the week.
Max has a weekly allowance only, with no daily cap.
When you’ve used up your allowance, on-demand credits keep you working without interruption. If your on-demand balance is also empty, sessions pause until your allowance refreshes or you purchase more credits.
If you have an eligible ChatGPT Go, Plus, or Pro subscription, you can also use your ChatGPT plan to pay for GPT model usage so it doesn’t count against your Devin allowance.

When you hit your limit
Free: Wait until your next daily or weekly reset.
Pro, Teams, or Max: Purchase extra usage to keep working without interruption.
